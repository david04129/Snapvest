Features

Designed for personal asset organization

More than bookkeeping, Walleaf brings accounts, allocation, investment performance, and transaction records back into one clear view.

All-in-One Asset Tracking

Across currencies and markets, all accounts and investments can be organized in one platform to show your complete asset picture.

Debts and Other Assets

Beyond stocks, crypto, and cash, any form of asset or liability can be recorded so your financial situation stays easy to understand.

Charts and Custom Groups

Trend charts, pie charts, and performance charts are included, with custom grouping that lets you build your own investment perspective.

No Login Required

Privacy without compromise. Your data stays on your device, with no account or password required and no full asset record exposed.

Automatic exchange-rate conversion makes multi-currency investing more intuitive, so you can understand asset value beyond one currency.

Simple and Minimal

Designed around intuitive use, Walleaf keeps the interface clean and uncomplicated so you can focus on investing instead of the tool.

Your ledger stays in your hands

Walleaf is designed around local storage. In everyday use, your accounts, transactions, cost basis, and debt records are primarily stored on your device.
