## Privacy Policy

大標題：Privacy Policy

開場內文：Walleaf ("the App", "we", or "us") values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information related to your use of the App.

開場內文：Walleaf is designed to minimize our access to your personal financial records. Under normal use, you do not need to provide your name, email address, or directly identifiable account information. Your account names, transaction records, holding costs, debts, other assets, and most preferences are primarily stored locally on your device.

開場內文：To provide stock prices, exchange rates, and market data updates, the App may send necessary query information to backend services, such as asset type, stock symbol, cryptocurrency symbol, or currency pair. This information is used to query third-party data sources and return results to the App.

開場內文：We do not receive or store your complete transaction history, account balances, holding costs, net worth details, or data that could reconstruct your personal ledger through normal bookkeeping use.

中標題：1. Scope

內文：This policy applies to the Walleaf iOS application, backend services connected for functions such as prices and exchange rates, and policy pages accessed through links in the App. It does not apply to the privacy practices of Apple, iCloud, brokerages, exchanges, quote providers, or other third-party services.

中標題：2. Information We Collect and Process

小標題：2.1 Information Stored on Your Device

內文：The following data is created and managed by you and is primarily stored locally on your device: account names, account types, currencies; transactions, holdings, costs, debts, and other asset records; interface preferences; and temporary demo data used in demo mode.

內文：If you export a backup file, you choose where to store and protect that file. We cannot access your iCloud account contents and cannot restore a lost backup on your behalf.

小標題：2.2 Information Sent to Backend Services

內文：To provide quotes, exchange rates, and service stability, Walleaf may process necessary information through third-party backend databases, API services, and market data sources, such as anonymous identifiers or access tokens, asset types, symbols, currencies, request time, status codes, and technical connection logs.

內文：This data does not include your complete account details, transaction records, holding costs, or personal net worth.

小標題：2.3 Device Permissions

內文：Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode is handled by iOS when you enable privacy lock. We do not collect or store biometric data. Photo access is used only when you choose to save shared chart images to your photo library.

小標題：2.4 Subscriptions and Payments

內文：Walleaf Plus subscriptions, payments, cancellations, and refunds are handled by the Apple App Store. We use Apple's mechanisms to verify subscription status and do not store your credit card number. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID and are separate from your local bookkeeping data.

中標題：3. How We Use Information

內文：We use the information described above to provide bookkeeping, asset summaries, gain/loss and trend displays; query and cache prices and exchange rates; support backup and restore; provide privacy lock, themes, and personalization; prevent abuse and maintain service security; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable laws or lawful authority requests.

中標題：4. Sharing and Third Parties

內文：We do not sell your personal data. To provide App functionality, we may use third-party backend databases, cloud services, quote and exchange-rate data sources, and Apple services for subscriptions and App distribution. These services are used only for necessary functions such as market data queries, subscription verification, service stability, and support.

中標題：5. Retention and Deletion

內文：Local data remains in the App sandbox until you delete the App, restore over it, or clear it yourself. Backup files are managed by you. Backend anonymous identifiers, API logs, symbol queries, and other technical data are retained as needed for operations and service settings, then deleted or anonymized.

中標題：6. Data Security

內文：We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect transmission and backend data, such as HTTPS, access controls, and rate limiting. However, internet transmission cannot be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

中標題：7. Children's Privacy

內文：The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

中標題：8. International Transfers

內文：Necessary data may be processed outside your region. We will take required measures under applicable laws, including GDPR, Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, and other applicable regulations.

中標題：9. Your Rights

內文：Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have rights to access, review, copy, supplement, correct, stop processing, or delete personal data. Because Walleaf does not store your complete ledger, these rights mainly apply to technical data needed by our backend.
